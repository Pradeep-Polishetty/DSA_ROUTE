Strategy

Pro Tips to Reach Hard Level

The difference between someone who plateaus at medium and someone who crushes hards is mostly strategy, not intelligence.

01
Solve by pattern, not randomly. “Two Sum → 3Sum → Container with Water” is a learning arc. Random shuffling wastes months.
02
2–3 problems daily beats weekend marathons. Spaced repetition matters. Your brain consolidates patterns during sleep.
03
After solving, always read top solutions. Your O(n²) may pass, but you need to see the O(n) approach — that's where the learning is.
04
Attempt every hard for 30 min first. The struggle before the hint builds the intuition that makes the next hard problem easier.
05
For DP: learn the 4-step framework. Identify subproblems → write recurrence → memoize top-down → convert to bottom-up tabulation.
06
Do LeetCode weekly contests from Phase 2. Timed pressure simulates real interviews. Your rank will improve dramatically month over month.
07
Re-solve problems you got wrong, after 3 days. If you can't reproduce the solution cold, you don't actually know it yet.
08
Write your solution before running it. Mentally trace through edge cases. This builds the debugging instinct that matters in interviews.
